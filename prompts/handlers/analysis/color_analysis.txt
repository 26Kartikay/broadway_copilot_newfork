## 🎨 Task: Seasonal Color Palette Identification

Analyze the uploaded portrait image and return the person's seasonal color palette as a JSON object.
User context: {user_context}

---

## STEP 1 — Quality Check

Reject ONLY if the image is genuinely unusable: extreme blur, near-total darkness, no face visible, or the subject is fully obscured.

IMPORTANT: Clear photos of real people — even if the lighting is slightly warm, slightly shadowed, or the face is at an angle — must NOT be rejected. Only reject truly broken or faceless images. If a face is visible and skin/hair/eyes can be assessed, set quality_ok: true and proceed.

---

## STEP 2 — Compensate for Image Lighting First

Before analysing anything else, assess the lighting conditions of the photo:

- If the photo has strong WARM lighting (orange/yellow studio light, golden hour, candlelight, warm cinematic filter) — mentally subtract that warmth. Warm lighting makes everyone look warmer than they are.
- If the photo has strong COOL lighting (blue studio, overcast daylight, cool flash) — mentally subtract that coolness.
- To find the true undertone, look at the SHADOW AREAS of the skin (shadows reveal undertone more reliably than highlights), the WHITES OF THE EYES (use as a neutral white-balance reference — if they look warm/yellow the whole image is warm-lit), and the LIP COLOR in its natural state.
- Only after compensating for lighting should you proceed to feature analysis.

---

## STEP 3 — Observe These 4 Signals

Assess each signal independently. Write out your internal observation for each before picking a palette.

### Signal A — UNDERTONE (most important signal)

Look at: lip color, shadow areas of skin, inner wrist veins if visible.

- WARM: peachy/golden/yellow/orange-toned lips, skin has a bronze or golden glow even in shadow, greenish veins
- COOL: pink/mauve/berry/rose lips, skin reads grey, pink, or bluish especially in shadows, blue-purple veins  
- NEUTRAL: neither clearly warm nor cool — features are blended, lips are neutral rose-brown, skin has no obvious golden or pink cast

⚠️ CRITICAL — do NOT confuse skin DEPTH with undertone:
- A person with very dark skin can have COOL undertones (grey-brown, blue-black tones) or WARM undertones (golden-brown, bronze tones)
- A person with medium tan/olive skin can be COOL (ashy olive) or WARM (golden olive)
- A person with fair skin can be WARM (peachy) or COOL (pinkish)
- Medium-brown, tan, or South Asian skin is NOT automatically warm. Check the lips and shadows.

### Signal B — DEPTH

How dark are the HAIR and EYES combined (ignore skin depth):
- LIGHT: light/medium hair + light/medium eyes
- MEDIUM: medium-dark hair + medium eyes, OR medium hair + dark eyes
- DEEP: very dark (near-black) hair AND very dark eyes together

### Signal C — CONTRAST

Imagine the photo in black and white. How different is the lightness of hair vs skin vs eyes?
- LOW: all features are similar in lightness — nothing dramatically stands out; features blend together
- MEDIUM: visible but not dramatic difference — hair is noticeably darker than skin but not stark
- HIGH: dramatic, striking difference — very dark hair on light skin, or very bright eyes/teeth that pop against dark features

### Signal D — CHROMA (Clarity vs Mutedness)

Do the person's features look VIVID and CLEAR, or MUTED and DUSTY?
- CLEAR/BRIGHT: features pop and are striking — eyes are vivid, skin glows, coloring has energy
- MUTED/SOFT: features blend and look dusty or greyed-down — like the saturation was turned down; nothing pops dramatically; the person would look washed out in very bright vivid colors

---

## STEP 4 — Follow This Decision Tree Strictly

### If WARM undertone → choose between SPRING and AUTUMN

**Is chroma CLEAR/BRIGHT?** → SPRING family:
- LIGHT depth + LOW contrast → `LIGHT_SPRING` (golden-fair, peachy, delicate — think fair-skinned with golden warmth and soft features)
- MEDIUM depth + MEDIUM contrast + golden glow → `TRUE_SPRING` (warm, golden, peachy, fresh — like Jennifer Lopez, Chris Hemsworth)
- HIGH contrast OR very vivid coloring → `BRIGHT_SPRING` (warm but striking — clear bright features with warm base)

**Is chroma MUTED/SOFT?** → AUTUMN family — now use DEPTH + CONTRAST to pick sub-palette:
- LOW-MEDIUM contrast + features look dusty/blended + LOW-MEDIUM depth → `SOFT_AUTUMN`
  (muted, earthy, nothing pops — like Aamir Khan, Emma Stone, Drew Barrymore, Shah Rukh Khan)
- MEDIUM contrast + GOLDEN RICHNESS visible + MEDIUM depth → `TRUE_AUTUMN`
  (rich, spiced, golden-brown — like Beyoncé, Robert Downey Jr, Jennifer Lopez)
- HIGH contrast + DEEP features (near-black hair AND dark eyes) + warm undertone confirmed → `DARK_AUTUMN`
  (deep, bronzy, dramatic warm — like Idris Elba, Oprah, Deepika Padukone)

### If COOL undertone → choose between SUMMER and WINTER

**Is chroma MUTED/SOFT?** → SUMMER family:
- LIGHT depth + very LOW contrast + everything light and soft → `LIGHT_SUMMER`
  (ash-fair, cool, ethereal, delicate — like Taylor Swift, Princess Diana)
- MEDIUM depth + MEDIUM contrast + ashy-cool features → `TRUE_SUMMER`
  (ash brown hair, pink-cool skin, grey/blue/green eyes — like Chris Evans, Kate Middleton, Hrithik Roshan)
- LOW-MEDIUM contrast + features blend + slightly warm-neutral lean but overall cool → `SOFT_SUMMER`
  (greyed-out, dusty cool, blended — like Brad Pitt, Katrina Kaif)

**Is chroma CLEAR/BRIGHT?** → WINTER family:
- HIGH contrast + COOL + MEDIUM depth (dark hair on fair-medium skin) → `TRUE_WINTER`
  (icy cool, high contrast — like Angelina Jolie, Aishwarya Rai, Tom Cruise, Keanu Reeves)
- HIGH contrast + COOL + VERY DEEP features (near-black skin AND hair AND eyes) → `DARK_WINTER`
  (deep, cool-neutral, dramatic — like Kylie Jenner in natural state)
- HIGH contrast + COOL + VERY VIVID/BRIGHT features (especially striking clear eyes) → `BRIGHT_WINTER`
  (electric clarity, icy bright — like Megan Fox)

### If NEUTRAL undertone → identify which signal dominates

- Predominantly MUTED + LOW contrast + any warmth → `SOFT_AUTUMN`
- Predominantly MUTED + LOW contrast + no warmth → `SOFT_SUMMER`
- LIGHT overall + any warmth → `LIGHT_SPRING`
- LIGHT overall + no warmth → `LIGHT_SUMMER`
- Otherwise use depth + contrast to pick the closest season above

## STEP 5 — Run All Anti-Bias Checks Before Finalising

### Check 1 — TRUE_AUTUMN gate (most over-predicted palette)
If you are about to output TRUE_AUTUMN, verify ALL THREE are true:
- ✅ Undertone is genuinely WARM — not just medium/tan/brown skin depth
- ✅ Contrast is MEDIUM — not high (→ DARK_AUTUMN), not low (→ SOFT_AUTUMN)
- ✅ Chroma is RICH and GOLDEN — not dusty/muted (→ SOFT_AUTUMN), not vivid/clear (→ TRUE_SPRING)
If any one of these fails → reconsider.

### Check 2 — SOFT_AUTUMN vs TRUE_AUTUMN (most confused pair)
The single deciding factor is CHROMA:
- TRUE_AUTUMN: warmth reads RICH, GOLDEN, ALIVE — like spiced honey or terracotta. Features glow.
- SOFT_AUTUMN: warmth reads DUSTY, MUTED, GREYED-DOWN — like dried herbs or weathered wood. Nothing pops. The person looks best in muted earthy tones, not vivid ones.
Ask yourself: does this person's coloring look RICH or does it look like the saturation was dialled down? Rich → TRUE_AUTUMN. Dialled down → SOFT_AUTUMN.

### Check 3 — DARK_AUTUMN vs DARK_WINTER at deep skin tones
Both have very deep features and high contrast. The ONLY differentiator is undertone warmth:
- DARK_AUTUMN: skin has WARM, BRONZY, GOLDEN or ORANGE-BROWN quality — glows in terracotta and olive
- DARK_WINTER: skin reads NEUTRAL-COOL, GREY-BROWN, BLUE-TONED or EBONY — no bronze warmth; ashy or cool depth
For deep skin: look at where the skin is lightest (inner arm, forehead) — does it glow golden (DARK_AUTUMN) or read neutral-grey-cool (DARK_WINTER)?

### Check 4 — South Asian / olive skin bias
Many South Asian people are SOFT_AUTUMN, SOFT_SUMMER, TRUE_SUMMER, or even TRUE_WINTER — not automatically TRUE_AUTUMN or DARK_AUTUMN. The surface skin colour alone tells you nothing about undertone. Treat every South Asian person's undertone as unknown until confirmed by lip colour and shadow reads.

### Check 5 — SOFT palette check
If the person's features look BLENDED and nothing stands out dramatically — no vivid eye colour, no richly saturated skin, no stark contrast, no features that pop — the answer is almost certainly SOFT_AUTUMN or SOFT_SUMMER, not any TRUE or DARK season. Both TRUE and DARK seasons require features that read with more presence and definition.

### Check 6 — Quality rejection gate
Are you about to set quality_ok: false? Only do so if the image is genuinely unusable — pure black, extreme motion blur, no face visible, or subject fully hidden. A celebrity photo, a slightly shadowed selfie, a warm-lit portrait, or a face at a mild angle must be accepted. When in doubt: accept and analyse.

```json

  "error_message": "Oops, can you try sending a clearer picture of your face? 💖",
  "inferred_gender": null,
  "inferred_age_group": null

```

**Output rules:**
- `palette_name` must be EXACTLY one of: `LIGHT_SPRING`, `TRUE_SPRING`, `BRIGHT_SPRING`, `LIGHT_SUMMER`, `TRUE_SUMMER`, `SOFT_SUMMER`, `SOFT_AUTUMN`, `TRUE_AUTUMN`, `DARK_AUTUMN`, `TRUE_WINTER`, `BRIGHT_WINTER`, `DARK_WINTER`
- `inferred_gender`: `MALE`, `FEMALE`, or `UNKNOWN`
- `inferred_age_group`: `TEEN`, `ADULT`, or `SENIOR`
- If quality_ok is false: palette_name, inferred_gender, and inferred_age_group must all be null
